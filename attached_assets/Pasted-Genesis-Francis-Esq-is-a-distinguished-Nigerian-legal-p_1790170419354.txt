Genesis Francis, Esq. is a distinguished Nigerian legal practitioner, Notary Public, and corporate governance expert with over Fourteen (14) years of post-call experience in litigation, corporate and commercial law, company secretarial practice, regulatory compliance, property law, contract negotiation, and legal advisory services.
He is the Principal Partner and Co-Founder of Hilltop Attorneys, a full-service law firm established in 2020. The firm has grown into a reputable legal practice with a strong international presence, operating from its head office in Abuja, Nigeria, and an office at Calgary, Canada. As Principal Partner, Genesis Francis heads the firm's Nigerian Office, providing strategic leadership for its operations, driving its vision, and overseeing the delivery of high-quality legal services to individuals, corporate organizations, government institutions, and international clients.
Genesis Francis has extensive experience in litigation, corporate advisory services, regulatory compliance, commercial transactions, company secretarial practice, and dispute resolution. He has successfully represented clients before superior courts of record, advised companies on corporate governance and regulatory compliance, negotiated and drafted commercial agreements, conducted legal due diligence, and provided strategic legal solutions to complex legal and business challenges.
His sound knowledge of the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020 (as amended), as well as other regulatory and commercial laws, has positioned him as a trusted adviser on corporate governance, compliance, and business structuring.
In recognition of his integrity, competence, and distinguished contribution to the legal profession, he was appointed a Notary Public by the Honourable Chief Justice of Nigeria in 2022.
Beyond legal practice, Genesis is a respected advocate for the rule of law, anti-corruption, and good governance. He currently serves as the National Director of the Directorate of Anti-Corruption, Rule of Law and Good Governance (DARG) of the Christian Lawyers Fellowship of Nigeria (CLASFON), where he provides strategic leadership in promoting accountability, judicial integrity, ethical governance, and institutional reforms.
A visionary legal practitioner and accomplished leader, Genesis Francis combines exceptional legal expertise with strategic leadership, advocacy, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills. He is committed to delivering innovative legal solutions, strengthening institutions, promoting corporate governance, and advancing the rule of law in Nigeria and across international jurisdictions
