AFROLANDX
Vendor Onboarding, Shipping & Marketing Plan

These operate the Nigeria → foreign buyer leg specifically (not just interstate delivery within Nigeria):

2. Vendor Acquisition — Route 1: Field Representatives
Locations & Payment
•	3 representatives, 1 each in Lagos, Aba, and Onitsha
•	Payment: ₦115,000 per rep (revised up from the initial ₦100,000 figure)
•	Target: up to 30 real, authentic, active vendors per rep — quality over volume (revised down from 100)
Phase 1 — Vendor Sourcing & Data Collection
•	Rep visits vendors in their location and confirms the products are good enough for the platform
•	Collects: vendor name, business name, phone number, email, and confirmation the business is registered with CAC
•	Submits collected data to Afrolandx to create the vendor account
•	Afrolandx admin reviews and approves the account, then emails the vendor a one-time password
•	Vendor logs in and changes the password on first login
Phase 2 — Product Listing (two possible approaches, to be decided)
•	Option A: Reps manually upload each vendor's products directly, then notify the vendor the listing is live so they can log in and edit
•	Option B: Reps only take/collect product photos on-site. An AI feature auto-generates the product title and description from the photos; the vendor only has to input a price. This requires Afrolandx to actually have this AI feature built — not confirmed yet, needs to be checked with the dev team before this route is planned around
Phase 2 was mentioned as a further ₦100,000 payment for handling roughly 100 vendors — this number should be reconciled with the revised 30-vendor cap from Phase 1 before finalizing, since the two figures don't currently match.
3. Vendor Acquisition — Route 2: Targeted Adverts
Second onboarding channel, running in parallel to the field reps. Facebook is the recommended primary platform given its reach in Nigeria; TikTok is a secondary option but depends on having strong visual content first. Proposed approach: commission micro-influencer videos, then run those videos as paid creative across Facebook and TikTok.
Micro-Influencer Budget
•	Proposed range: ₦1,000,000–1,500,000 to get 2 micro-influencers to create content about Afrolandx
•	This is an estimate, not a confirmed cost — since Afrolandx is a new platform, there's no guarantee influencers at this budget level will take the offer, and the actual rate needed is unconfirmed until outreach happens
Ad Costing — Per Location, Duration & Reach
Based on published Nigeria Meta ads benchmarks (CPC ₦30–500, CPM ₦450–5,000, cost per lead ₦500–5,000+). These are directional estimates only — Afrolandx has no historical ad account data yet, so actual lead-to-onboarded-vendor conversion is unconfirmed until a real campaign runs.
Tier	Budget per Location	Duration	Estimated Reach	Estimated Leads	Est. Vendors Onboarded
Test	₦150,000–200,000	14 days	~100,000–130,000 impressions	60–120 leads	~10–25 (assumes ~20% lead-to-vendor conversion)
Recommended	₦300,000–400,000	14–21 days	~200,000–260,000 impressions	120–250 leads	~25–50
Aggressive (to approach 100)	₦500,000–700,000+	21–30 days	~330,000–460,000 impressions	200–450 leads	~40–90, still not guaranteed at 100
Meta requires roughly 7-14 days minimum per campaign to exit its learning phase and optimize properly — shorter campaigns will underperform these estimates. 7.5% VAT applies on top of all ad spend.
Reaching 100 onboarded vendors per location through ads alone, at a realistic budget, is not guaranteed — worth planning for ads to supplement the field reps rather than replace them.
Platform Requirement for This Route
Vendors coming in through ads have no rep walking them through onboarding, so the self-serve flow has to be smooth end-to-end: name, business name, business registration (CAC) number with verification, phone number, and any other required fields, completed without assistance.
4. Shipping Flow & Shipping Verification Agents
Flow: Vendor → Shipping Agent (verifies the product) → Logistics/freight company (e.g. CargoNaija) → Foreign buyer.
Shipping Agents
•	3 agents, one in each of the same locations as the vendor reps: Lagos, Aba, Onitsha
•	Role is virtual-assistant style: receive the product from the vendor, verify it matches the listing, then hand off to the logistics company for onward shipping
•	Proposed pay: ~₦100,000 — the pay period (monthly vs. per task) wasn't specified and should be confirmed before finalizing
•	Logistics firms would be contacted to arrange pickup directly from these agents, rather than from individual vendors
Platform Requirement for This Role
This role doesn't currently have a system to work from and needs one built, similar in concept to how Jumia verifies products before they ship. Needed: a dashboard where the agent can see incoming products, confirm receipt/arrival, mark an item as verified and ready to ship, and log all of this against the order.
5. Policies Required Before Vendor Onboarding
Currently missing from the platform and needed before vendors are onboarded through any route:
•	Seller's/Vendor Policy
•	Return Policy
•	Shipping Policy
•	Refund Policy
•	Dispute Resolution Policy
•	Privacy Policy
Vendors should be required to read and accept these as part of the onboarding flow, whether they're onboarded by a rep or self-serve through ads.
6. Platform Build Requirements — Consolidated
Pulling together everything referenced above that needs to exist or be confirmed before going to market. Flagged as build/confirm items rather than assumed to already be in place:
•	Self-serve vendor onboarding form: name, business name, CAC number with verification, phone number, email
•	Admin approval workflow with auto-generated OTP email and forced password change on first login
•	AI feature to auto-generate product title/description from uploaded photos (for Phase 2, Option B) — needs confirmation from the dev team on whether this exists or needs to be built
•	Shipping agent dashboard: view incoming products, confirm receipt, mark ready-to-ship, verification log tied to each order
•	Published policy pages (seller, return, shipping, refund, dispute, privacy) with a mandatory acceptance step in onboarding
Basic launch requirements should be built first, in phases, before scaling either the field rep or ad-based vendor acquisition routes.
