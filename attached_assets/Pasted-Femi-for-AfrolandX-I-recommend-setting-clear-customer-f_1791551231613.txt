Femi, for AfrolandX, I recommend setting clear, customer-friendly policies that are realistic for an Africa-wide marketplace. Since AfrolandX manages orders, shipping, refunds and vendor settlements centrally, your policies should distinguish between product refunds and shipping refunds.

## Recommended AfrolandX settings

Business address

Use your actual registered business address as the official address. If you operate online without a public storefront, display your registered office or business correspondence address rather than inventing a physical location.

Customer support hours

## Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM WAT

West Africa Time (UTC+1)

This gives customers predictable support hours while keeping staffing manageable. You can offer automated support outside these hours.

Refund processing timeline

## 5–14 business days

Use this as the estimated time for refunds to reach the original payment method after approval and initiation. Payment providers and banks may take longer.

Returns and shipping refunds

* Acknowledge refund requests within 1–2 business days.

* Aim to review requests within 3 business days after receiving the necessary evidence.

* Refund eligible shipping charges when the order was not delivered due to a seller or logistics failure, subject to applicable law and the published policy.
