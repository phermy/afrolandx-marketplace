---
name: Verified review provenance
description: Why older order feedback must not automatically become public verified reviews
---

Do not bulk-approve historical order feedback solely because it has an order and product reference. A public verified review needs server-confirmed ownership, a paid delivered order containing the product, and a real moderation decision.

**Why:** The previous feedback submission path accepted client-supplied order and product IDs without proving that the signed-in person bought the item, and it awarded points at submission. An old reference alone cannot establish review provenance.

**How to apply:** When migrating, importing, moderating, or summarizing reviews, keep older records out of the published set unless their provenance has been independently checked. Never infer verified purchase from a matching identifier alone.
