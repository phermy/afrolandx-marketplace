---
name: Event planner commission
description: Commission base and financial safety decision for event planning services
---

Apply Afrolandx's 8% event commission only to the planner's own service fee for a customer-confirmed, successfully completed event. Do not charge it on venue, hotel, food, clothing, or other third-party costs, even if those appear in the planner's quotation.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the planner-fee-only basis. Supplier amounts can be estimates, paid outside the platform, and are not evidence of completed platform sales. Marketplace product commission remains a separate merchandise-only calculation.

**How to apply:** Show the planning fee and third-party estimates as separate figures. Require customer confirmation before recognizing commission. Do not activate online event-fee collection or imply planner payouts are available until event-specific refund, dispute, payout, and reconciliation processes are implemented and tested; a quoted amount or commission receivable is not proof that money was collected.

Keep event planner applicant review and commission records in private administration, not on the customer-facing Events page. Do not display operational commission-accounting explanations to customers.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for the administration content to be private and removed from the public-facing event experience.

**How to apply:** New event administration controls and ledger explanations belong behind admin access; retain appropriate customer-facing clarity about actual payment availability.
