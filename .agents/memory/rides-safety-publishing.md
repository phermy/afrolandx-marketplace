---
name: Rides safety publishing
description: Rules for turning AfrolandX Rides safety and support guidance into public content.
---

Public Rides safety guidance must distinguish confirmed support channels and capabilities from conditional or planned features. Never publish bracketed placeholders, unsupported response-time promises, or invented emergency-service numbers.

**Why:** The source guidance mixes current instructions with conditional feature language and unfinished contact placeholders. Safety content can cause harm if users rely on a channel or capability that is not operational.

**How to apply:** Keep immediate-danger guidance directed to local emergency services first. Use only confirmed AfrolandX channels, preserve “where available” wording for conditional tools, and verify any new contact details before displaying them publicly.
