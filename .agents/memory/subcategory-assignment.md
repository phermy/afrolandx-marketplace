---
name: Subcategory assignment
description: Why new shopping sections are intentionally empty until vendors assign their products
---

New subcategories should only include listings deliberately assigned by their seller; do not infer assignments from existing product names, descriptions, or ordinary free-text tags.

**Why:** The user approved adding smaller shopping labels under existing categories without inventing or misrepresenting inventory. Text-based guesses could place unrelated older products in culturally specific sections, so an empty section is more honest.

**How to apply:** When expanding the category taxonomy or improving discovery, preserve the distinction between exact assigned subcategories and loose keyword search. Keep existing products in their parent categories until their vendors explicitly classify them.
