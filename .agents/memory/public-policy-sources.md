name: Public policy source handling
description: Rules for turning the supplied Afrolandx policy documents into customer-facing legal pages.
---

Use “Afrolandx,” September 4, 2026, CAC REG: 7039498, and info@afrolandx.com where the supplied documents contain the corresponding approved placeholders. Do not guess unresolved commercial or legal terms such as settlement cadence, settlement hold periods, fees, addresses, phone numbers, or a standalone Refund Policy.

**Why:** The uploaded policy set contains placeholder text and references a Refund Policy that was not supplied. Publishing guessed terms would create commitments the business has not approved.

**How to apply:** Preserve substantive duties and deadlines from the source documents. Until an approved standalone Refund Policy exists, direct refund questions to the Return Policy and Complaints and Dispute Resolution Policy without creating a dead legal link.

Approval of the support hours and refund crediting estimate in the recommendation note does not approve its other suggested enquiry, acknowledgement or review targets.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected only the two settings presented for approval. The note includes alternative timelines and additional recommendations, not adopted business commitments.

**How to apply:** Keep those additional service targets unpublished unless separately approved. Paystack distinguishes initiation, processing and customer receipt; its public refund documentation says customer receipt can take up to 10 business days even after the processor marks a refund processed. Describe the approved range as an estimate, never a guaranteed date.
