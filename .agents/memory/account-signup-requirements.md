---
name: Account signup requirements
description: Required phone collection and the distinction between vendor signup and approval
---

The user requires a phone number when creating all accounts, especially vendors. Existing accounts without a phone should remain accessible; the request concerns new registration.

**Why:** The user found that new customer accounts had no required phone and administrators lacked useful customer contact details.

**How to apply:** Require phone on both customer and vendor signup, while showing missing historical data honestly rather than inventing contact information.

Vendor signup must visibly lead into the business application, including after email verification or a later login. Choosing vendor signup is an application intent, not permission to sell.

**Why:** The user reported that the vendor signup button simply created a customer account without a functioning vendor path. Existing approval and CAC safeguards must remain intact.

**How to apply:** Preserve the signup choice across verification without granting vendor or elevated roles from registration input.
