Use plain task labels and neutral, task appropriate icons for assisted features. Do not market features with “AI” names or sparkle branding. Keep clear, accurate AI disclosures in explanatory text.

**Why:** The user requested this distinction as part of improving trust and content, not removing disclosure of AI use.

**How to apply:** Apply to new and revised shopper, vendor and admin features. Describe what an action does in its name; explain AI involvement, review requirements and relevant privacy limitations nearby. Preserve truthful technical, accessibility and biographical references.
