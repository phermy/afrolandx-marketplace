name: Registration visual direction
description: User feedback on the Afrolandx signup design direction and legibility priority.
---

Keep the Afrolandx registration page's confident split layout with a dark brand area, warm textile-inspired artwork, and a clean light form area, while ensuring every line of copy stays readable against its background.

**Why:** The user said the overall design looked great but called attention to wording that was difficult to read where artwork and small type reduced clarity.

**How to apply:** For future iterations, retain the accepted visual direction and check short desktop and mobile viewports for text contrast, type size, and decorative overlap before changing the design substantially.
