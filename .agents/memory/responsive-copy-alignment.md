---
name: Responsive copy alignment
description: How to interpret the request for well-justified wording across screen sizes without damaging readability.
---

Use natural start alignment for short interface copy and narrow phone or tablet columns. Reserve fully justified alignment for long-form copy in wide reading columns.

**Why:** Forced justification stretched short sentences and created uneven spaces at tablet widths; the goal is clean, readable wording across screens rather than literal justification everywhere.

**How to apply:** Review actual text layout at phone, tablet, and desktop widths when adding copy or changing global typography. Do not impose blanket justification on all paragraphs or list items.
