Keep Discover hidden from customers and visitors while allowing admins to inspect the internal preview. Do not re-enable public navigation, direct page access, inquiries, place browsing, or new Discover booking and subscription charges as incidental work.

**Why:** The user explicitly deferred Discover for future development rather than launching its unfinished travel and booking flows now. Existing payment verification may still be needed for transactions initiated before access was restricted.

**How to apply:** Treat public launch as a deliberate separate project decision. Before restoring access, verify actual supplier booking/fulfillment, cancellation and refund handling, and customer-facing checkout claims; keep historical payment reconciliation reachable where necessary.
