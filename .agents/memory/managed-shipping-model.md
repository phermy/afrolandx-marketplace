The user chose Afrolandx managed shipping: the customer pays Afrolandx and the vendor prepares and hands over the package. The current direction is a marketplace on a shipping aggregator, comparing Shipbubble and Terminal Africa and keeping one as backup. Do not build a Temu-style warehouse operation. Existing partner access remains invitation only and limited to assigned shipments.

**Why:** The user wants customers to have one place for payment, tracking and help, and explicitly chose aggregator-based marketplace shipping rather than warehouse operations.

**How to apply:** Retain central Afrolandx oversight with full shipping access for verified Admins and package-level tracking. Use aggregator APIs for rates, delivery dates, labels and tracking once genuine account access is confirmed. Never present manual updates as live carrier integration or a payment record as an actual transfer. Preserve historical shipping and agency-payment records.
