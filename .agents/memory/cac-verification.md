---
name: CAC vendor verification
description: Why vendor registry checks are a documented manual process, not automated lookup
---

Vendor CAC checks should use the official CAC Public Search with an administrator comparing the registration number, registered name, and active listing status. Do not describe formatting checks or an administrator's checkbox as automatic government verification.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose public-search admin review because they do not have access to CAC's authorized VAS service. A plausible number can belong to an unrelated business, and there is no approved automated data source for this project.

**How to apply:** Keep seller approval dependent on a recorded official-search review. If authorized CAC VAS access is later provided, add a separate integration and clearly distinguish API-verified records from historical manual reviews.

New vendor approvals require private CAC evidence and proof of authority, followed by two distinct, verified staff reviewers; neither may be the applicant. Possible duplicates require a documented resolution, not an automatic rejection.

**Why:** The user requested evidence uploads, a second approver and duplicate flags. A shared registration may need investigation rather than an unsupported assumption of fraud.

**How to apply:** Treat uploaded evidence as private business records, never public product media. Relevant draft edits invalidate earlier checks. Do not retroactively suspend approved vendors merely because the new requirements exist; use the staff monitoring queue to collect missing evidence and document concerns. Monitoring signals prompt review, not automatic penalties.
